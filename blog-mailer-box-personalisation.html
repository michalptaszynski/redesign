In this article we dig into what the Mailer Box can now be configured to do — and how to rebuild your favourite combination in the new set-up.

Mailer Box personalisation options

The range now spans box size, material, print and return options.

At Packhelp we are all about custom boxes. You probably know that already. However, our most beloved product — the Mailer Box — was lagging behind in this one respect. We couldn’t leave it like that, so we introduced a few changes that deliver a far more personalised experience.

  • The quantity — the price goes down when you buy in bulk
  • The size — choose from 11 sizes, or request a quote for a custom one
  • Print colour — pick from different colour saturations
  • The material — for the inside and the outside of the box
  • Print coverage — inside, outside, or maybe both
  • Adhesive stripes — none, one or two, for an easy return process

Some of you have been with us a long time and have a favourite Mailer Box configuration. So here is how to set each one up again — and what else is now within reach.

One Colour print

One Colour boxes are the first step towards adding a personal touch.

Choose from the CMYK colour chart to make sure the box fits your brand image.

One Colour Mailer Box

A sleek cardboard mailing box that nails the minimalist aesthetic — clean, simple, but a step above plain. The balance of style and budget for brands that want custom packaging without going over the top.

  • One colour from the whole CMYK chart, white excluded.
  • The colour outside can differ from the one inside — effectively two colours per box.
  • Optional single or double adhesive strip for a comfortable e-commerce experience.
  • Prints on white or kraft cardboard.

White Colour Mailer Box

An all-time customer favourite, and that is no exaggeration. A classy look, white contrasting against brown cardboard. Simple, yet so effective.

  • High-quality white print in water-based ink that really pops on the kraft box.
  • Optional single or double adhesive strip for ordering and returning online.
  • A natural but elegant look that never goes out of style.

Multicolour print

Want more colours on your box? There are three options, depending on your budget.

Multicolour Muted Mailer Box

The most affordable iteration of the multicolour boxes. If you are going for a minimalistic or rustic design in earthy, soft colours, this is the one. The testliner material is made of recycled pulp, which gives it a natural greyish tone — a surface that makes subdued colours look their best.

  • The most affordable way into a multicolour box.
  • Works best with light and soft colours.
  • Single or double adhesive strip available for e-commerce packages.

Multicolour Saturated Mailer Box

A vibrant upgrade. Made with whitened, uncoated cardboard, it offers a brighter canvas for designs that need a little extra pop. Want to print inside and out? Go for it.

  • The golden ratio between Muted and Premium, in both price and print saturation.
  • Multicolour print all over the box, outside and inside.
  • E-commerce ready with one or two adhesive stripes.

Multicolour Premium Mailer Box

The boldest option in the line-up. High-resolution digital printing brings designs through in rich, saturated colour — crisp, detailed, impossible to miss. Add a matte or gloss foil finish to dial it up further.

  • The most vivid, saturated print available, all over the box.
  • A choice of glossy, matt or no foil finish.
  • Multiple colours on a single box.
  • E-commerce adhesive stripes available.

The potential of bespoke packaging

The shift to e-commerce has changed the customer journey.

Unlike shopping in a store, where a product can be picked up and examined, online shoppers rely on images alone. That absence of touch raises the stakes on the first physical encounter: the arrival of the package.

Packaging isn’t just about protecting goods; it is about crafting an experience. And the unboxing has grown from a delivery into a marketing channel of its own.

50%

of UK shoppers name the unboxing experience as important — packaging has moved from logistical necessity to a measure of customer satisfaction.

52%

higher likelihood of a repurchase among shoppers who receive bespoke packaging.

200+

unboxing videos have passed a million views each — packaging as shareable content, not just a protective layer.

60M

hours spent watching unboxing videos, which makes packaging a form of digital entertainment in its own right.
