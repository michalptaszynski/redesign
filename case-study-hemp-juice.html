How Hemp Juice stayed true to its eco-mission and lowered its packaging costs by 25% with Packhelp

Meet Hemp Juice: a D2C brand specialising in hemp-based oils and cosmetics, competing in a crowded CBD market on quality, visual communication, and branding.

The brief was demanding: a diverse product range, precise branding requirements, a tight budget, and logistics that couldn’t get in the way of growth. So, how did Hemp Juice manage to cover all their packaging needs and cut costs by 25% at the same time? Here’s how.

Objectives

  • Optimise packaging costs and logistics
  • Meet high aesthetic standards across a diverse product range
  • Support the brand’s eco-mission

Results

  • A 25% cut in production, 3PL storage & fulfilment costs
  • Best quality-to-price ratio with matt foils and offset printing
  • Recycled materials and natural ink used throughout

The challenge

Hemp Juice’s range consists of six types of CBD oils and cosmetics, along with products for pets.

Each product differs in CBD concentration and health properties, and each oil type has its own visual character. The 30ml bottles ship in full-colour printed product boxes — nine different designs in total — yet the entire range still had to read as one coherent brand. Nothing less than perfect would do.

Eco-friendly printing

Natural, water-based inks throughout the range.

Flexibility

A delivery calendar and on-demand ordering with a Packhelp Plan.

Design

Matt foil finish paired with offset printing.

Hemp Juice was a growing company that needed logistic support just as much as it needed design — one without the other wouldn’t have solved the brief.

The solution

The Packhelp team went to work.

The product boxes were produced in GC1 cardboard at 400gsm, across nine different designs sized 3.5 × 3.5 × 11.1cm, each finished with a matt foil application. Shipping was handled with eco-friendly mailing boxes, cushioned with a natural filler instead of plastic.

The ordering & logistics process was made simple & seamless with a Packhelp Plan.

Image placeholder

The result

The end result was a truly original product box with a unique design over the entire surface.

The matt foil brought out the colour across all nine designs, while the overall project cut Hemp Juice’s packaging costs by 25%. Shipping got the same treatment: bio poly mailers made from vegetable starch, certified “OK compost INDUSTRIAL,” wrapped around boxes made from 90% recycled cardboard.

9

Graphic designs — nine product boxes, each reflecting a different product, with an extra layer of matte foil for more vivid colour.

25%

Lower costs — Packhelp’s in-house storage and fulfilment hub consolidated costs and enabled flexible ordering.

90%

Recycled content — the share of recycled material in Hemp Juice’s mailer boxes.

“When developing the design brief, we thought about two aspects: functionality and ecology. We wanted the mailer box to be visually attractive and encourage people to give it a second life. We know it worked because our customers often write that they use our box to keep jewellery, cosmetics or other items in it. You can’t ask for more than that.”

Radek Budzik, CEO, Hemp Juice

Why Hemp Juice chose Packhelp

Hemp Juice says the biggest benefit of working with Packhelp is a fully customised Packhelp Plan.

Bulk production, warehouse storage, shipping at set intervals, and fast turnaround on-demand — all wrapped into one seamless process, so Hemp Juice never has to choose between cost, speed, and consistency.

About Hemp Juice

Hemp Juice is a Polish brand producing and selling CBD oils and hemp-based products.

Its founders believe in CBD’s therapeutic potential, and the brand is defined by quality ingredients, distinctive styling, and a focus on ecological values and customer education.
