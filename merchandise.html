Branded merchandise for every day and every occasion

Packhelp supplies branded merchandise for businesses that want their logo on more than a box. We personalise clothing, drinkware, bags and office supplies with your logo and brand colours, using printing and engraving methods suited to each material. Order for staff uniforms, client gifts, a conference giveaway or a one-off product launch, with the same account and the same editor you already use for packaging.

Practical gadgets for the office and home

Notebooks, pens, power banks and desk organisers are the merchandise people actually keep. We stock office supplies and tech accessories that stay on a desk long after an event badge is thrown away, alongside home accessories like candles and plant pots for a softer, less corporate gift. Each item ships with your logo applied, ready to hand out or include in a welcome kit.

Apparel people wear with pride

Hoodies, tees and caps only work as merchandise if people actually want to wear them. Our clothing range covers everyday staff uniforms and streetwear-style drops alike, in sizes from XS to XXL, customised with embroidery, screen print or heat transfer depending on the fabric and the design.

Elegant gifts for clients and partners

Corporate gifting calls for a different finish than a giveaway tote. Drinkware, candles and travel accessories in our range are suited to laser engraving or debossing for a subtler, premium mark instead of a large printed logo — a better fit for client gifts, executive onboarding kits or holiday sends.

Sets and packaging for your merchandise

A single branded item is easy to lose in a drawer — a set, unboxed together, is what people remember. Our merchandise packaging and sets category brings a welcome kit box, branded gift wrap or a custom mailer sleeve together with the items inside, so a new hire's kit or a client gift arrives as one designed moment, not a loose pile of swag.

Branding techniques — from embroidery to laser engraving

Different materials take branding differently: embroidery and screen print for fabric, laser engraving and debossing for wood, metal and leather, heat transfer and UV printing for tech and plastics. We match the technique to the product during checkout, so a design that works on a tote bag doesn't get force-fit onto a steel water bottle.
