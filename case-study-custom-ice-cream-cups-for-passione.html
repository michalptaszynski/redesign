The Power of Customisation and Eco-Conscious Choices

Our ice cream cups are fully customisable, allowing you to design the entire surface with your chosen colours, patterns, and prints, perfectly reflecting your brand identity.

But what exactly is BIO-coating, and why should you consider it?

Understanding Coating Options: BIO vs. PE

The traditional PE-coating:

The ice cream cup industry has long relied on PE coatings. These coatings form a tough, water-resistant barrier that effectively protects contents. Their versatility and effectiveness have made them a popular choice for a wide range of food packaging, including ice cream cups.

However, a major drawback of PE coatings is that they are not biodegradable. This has become a significant concern for today’s environmentally conscious business owners.

Enter BIO-coatings, the sustainable alternative:

Bio-based coatings, derived from natural materials like sugarcane, offer a perfect eco-friendly alternative to traditional PE coatings. These cups are plastic-free and biodegradable, yet remain sturdy and greaseproof. They can withstand freezing temperatures and prevent oils and liquids from leaking out. Bio-coated cups are a great option for environmentally focused brands.

We offer both Bio-coated and PE-coated cups that can be fully customised with colours and patterns across the entire surface.
