This Christmas, make your gifts truly special.

We’re proud to work with businesses that pour their heart and soul into crafting unique, high-quality products. From artisanal foods and handcrafted jewellery to cosy home decor and self-care essentials, our clients offer a range of thoughtful gifts perfect for everyone on your list.

In this gift guide, we’ve curated our top picks across five categories: Hobbies, Self Care, Fashion & Jewellery, Food & Drinks and Home & Decor.

Shop small and spread joy this holiday season!

Hobbies

For creative minds, artistic souls and activity enthusiasts.

Discover the perfect gifts to support them in their passions.

1. Muno Puzzle

Unique puzzles with paintings by famous artists. Excellent entertainment for long evenings.

2. Rúngne Apparel

Professional climbing gear - chalk, chalk bags, tape and multi-use clothing. Functional and stylish.

3. Santa Film

Colour Negative Film for analogue photographers. The film results in fine grain and medium saturation, giving beautiful photos.

4. TAN LINE

Sustainable, recycled and very fashionable cycling and endurance sports gear and accessories.

5. HipKnitShop

Colourful yarns, knitting patterns, DYI crochet kits and various accessories. Perfect for the winter evenings.

6. Det Lilla Köksbryggeriet

Ready-made DYI kombucha kit with all the necessary components you need. Classic tart taste of tea and apple.

7. Notable Notebooks

Unique collections of designer stationery, writing and desk accessories. Gift someone the perfect planner for 2026.

8. V de Vikinga

Fun and modern DIY embroidery kits. Embroider your own, colourful cap, tote bag or t-shirt.

9. BAVVIC

Innovative, multifunctional building blocks designed by an architect - the perfect wooden toys for a gift.

10. Sailor Strap

Handmade, colourful and premium quality camera cords that keep it safe and looking great.

Self Care

Perfect gifts for anyone who appreciates a moment of relaxation.

Discover high-quality products that will bring the priceless gift of a home spa wellness experience.

1. Cream.ly

A complete, natural skincare routine in a minimal number of fun-looking products.

2. La Bomba Bath

Handmade, vegan and natural body cosmetics and colourful bath bombs that are environmentally friendly and smell incredibly.

3. STENDERS

Products for an exceptional bath ritual inspired by Nordic nature and ingredients.

4. Biotika

Perfumes in oil, essential oils, fragrance mists, as well as soy candles - all about the fragrance.

5. Orris

Luxurious soaps made from the highest quality plant-based ingredients with beautiful herbal fragrances.

6. Gitti

Innovative, plant-based and cruelty-free nail polishes with high performance in terms of brilliant shine and durability.

7. Xlash

Iconic eyelash serum that naturally and gently promotes lasting hair growth and health.

8. Copenhagen Grooming

Innovative male grooming and skincare. The Beard Growth Kit enhances beard growth by combining microneedling with active ingredients.

9. Sord

Comprehensive care for men. The Signature Set is a self-care kit for every man, containing five essential and effective products.

10. Feschi

Professional, vegan and cruelty-free hair cosmetics and accessories.

Fashion & Jewellery

For style icons, trendsetters and accessory lovers.

Help them highlight their unique style and make an impression this Christmas with these beautiful pieces.

1. KUYICHI

Clothing, especially denim pieces, made from organic and recycled materials. Vegan, timeless, and made to last.

2. Laser Barcelona

Unique collection made in collaboration between Laser Barcelona and different illustrators from all over the world.

3. ANEMA

Premium, distinctive, sustainable bags made with fabrics with minimal environmental impact.

4. Tropicfeel

Innovative travel backpacks, accessories, footwear and apparel made with sustainable approach and vegan materials.

5. IZIPIZI

High-quality sunglasses, reading glasses and blue light glasses which come in a variety of shapes and colours.

6. Treas

A handmade, crocheted bag from sustainable brands market, which offers a variety of unique clothing, jewellery and accessories.

7. Highsnobiety

Unique garments created in collaboration between Highsnobiety and various Parisian brands, pop-ups and creative individuals.

8. Rosanna de la Riva

Jewellery that is individually handcrafted using traditional techniques. One-of-a-kind earrings, bracelets, necklaces and more.

9. Happy Socks

The most colourful and fun socks on the market, available in countless designs and motifs.

10. Alma Ireland

Personalised stainless steel jewellery with the option to engrave a message of your choice.

Food & Drinks

For food lovers, home cooks and sweet tooths.

Discover delicious treats and drinks that will delight their eyes and tastebuds.

1. Djuce

Europe's best wines innovatively encapsulated in artist-designed cans that have a much smaller carbon footprint than glass bottles.

2. Solberg&Hansen;

Sustainably sourced coffee and tea from the oldest speciality coffee roaster and tea distributor in Norway.

3. Douze

Refreshing, non-alcoholic drinks with adaptogens, vitamins and probiotics to reduce stress, boost mood and improve health.

4. Wild Child Cacao

100% pure ceremonial grade cacao bar made to create pure and healthy cacao drinks with hot water or milk easily.

5. ROY Kombucha

High-quality, organic and healthy kombucha available in a range of interesting flavours enclosed in colourful cans.

6. Neptune Elements

A drink based on algae harvested in Brittany that offers an alternative to coffee and tea. It helps fight fatigue and stress and improves immunity.

7. Teasome

Carefully selected premium teas from Asian and African cultivation and the necessary brewing accessories.

8. Sweet Spot Kaffee

Wide selection of speciality coffees from different parts of the world, as well as top-quality chocolate to complement the coffee gift.

9. Browniegod

Freshly handcrafted brownies made from the finest Belgian chocolate. The brand gives an option to add a personalised, edible message or a photo to the brownie.

10. Matcha Gracias

An online shop a physical café with high-quality matcha tea in all its drinkable and edible versions, as well as matcha-making accessories.

Home & Decor

Gifts that create a warm and inviting atmosphere, adding a touch of charm and sophistication to any home.

Discover unique pieces that make every space feel special.

1. Ślady

Local, artisanal products inspired by the Baltic Sea, such as posters, ceramics and blankets.

2. ENDE Ceramics

Elegant, handmade ceramic objects for everyday use that combine functionality, original form and high-quality materials.

3. Jaskółka

Nature-inspired soy candles made with organic soy wax, natural wick, and vegan fragrance oils.

4. KALSTORE

Classic analogue wall calendars available in white or black. They will not only help to organise time but also decorate the wall with their minimalist elegance.

5. Mudita

Simple, minimalist and high-quality watches and phones that fulfil their basic functions and defy the modern technologies that often control people's lives.

6. Mindful Potter

Ceramic mugs, plates, bowls, etc., which are handmade from start to finish using stoneware clay and raw materials.

7. Maison Sarah Lavoine

A unique, iconic candle, entirely made of wax. Available in several colours and sizes. It will add elegance and cosiness to the interior.

8. Släppa

An eco-friendly, plastic-free solution for a clean home - reusable glass bottle and fizzy cleaning tablets that dissolve in water.

9. Boogie Bougie

Unusual-looking candles in aluminous containers that catch the eye. The candles are soy, handmade and available in many scents and colours.

That's it for this year's Holiday Gift Guide!

Whether you’re shopping for a sportsperson, a foodie, a fashion lover, or someone who loves a little self-care, these special finds will bring joy and make the season brighter. By choosing gifts from Packhelp’s clients, you’re not just giving a present—you’re supporting passionate creators and their amazing work.

Happy gifting! HoHoHo!
