Protect your products with sturdy construction

The Custom Mailer Box is built with one job in mind: keeping what's inside safe. Choose from three types of corrugated cardboard — standard, bleached or coated — each available in weights from 358 gsm to 510 gsm, so you can match rigidity and strength to your specific products and keep them secure through transport and storage. Precision manufacturing with a dimensional tolerance of ±2–4mm means every box fits exactly as designed.

Fit every shipment, every time

Whether you're shipping small accessories or bulkier items, the Custom Mailer Box comes in small, medium and large sizes, so you can match the box to the contents. Every variant fits standard courier and parcel-locker size brackets — InPost, DPD, Royal Mail, DHL — which keeps logistics simple and shipping costs down.

Create standout designs with full print coverage

Print options range from a clean one-colour design, through white ink, to full-colour artwork in muted, saturated or premium tones. Decide whether to print outside only, inside only, or both — giving you full creative control to build a consistent brand experience visible from the moment the box is opened.

Choose your finish and functionality

Round off the look with a matte or gloss foil finish that reinforces the surface as well as the aesthetic. Add a single or double self-adhesive strip to streamline packing and opening — especially useful for return shipments.

Build recognition from the very first touch

With full personalisation, your mailer box becomes a business card for your brand — creating a memorable unboxing moment and building customer loyalty.
